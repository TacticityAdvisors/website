Investments

Capital and diligence, built on regulatory judgment most investors do not have in-house.

Tacticity Capital is backed by our own family office and invests in healthcare, medtech, IVD, and SaMD companies at the angel and early stages, typically deploying ₹25 lakh to ₹5 crore per investment. Every investment and diligence decision is informed directly by former U.S. FDA reviewer judgment, the same expertise that underpins Tacticity Advisors' regulatory practice. This is not capital with regulatory consulting attached. It is capital that starts from a regulatory read of the opportunity.

01 · Angel Investing
First capital, with a regulatory read before the term sheet.
We back founders at the pre-seed and seed stage, where the regulatory pathway is often still being assumed rather than actually assessed. Our process reverses that: a device or diagnostic gets a genuine pathway and predicate read before capital moves, not after.
  • Regulatory pathway assessed before capital is committed

    Before any term sheet, we assess the likely FDA pathway, classification, and predicate landscape for the device. Founders get a straight answer on regulatory risk as part of diligence, not a surprise six months after the round closes.

  • Founder access to regulatory strategy from day one

    Portfolio founders get direct access to regulatory guidance from the moment capital is deployed, not after a Series A when the product architecture is already locked in and expensive to change.

  • Backed by our own family office

    Capital is deployed with the backing of our own family office, giving portfolio companies a stable, long-horizon investor rather than a fund working against a fixed exit clock. Typical check sizes range from ₹25 lakh to ₹5 crore.

  • Co-investment alongside angel groups and syndicates

    We participate alongside other angels and syndicates rather than requiring exclusivity, and bring regulatory diligence into the round for every investor at the table, not just ourselves.

02 · Early-Stage Investing
Growth capital where regulatory risk is the largest unpriced variable.
At Series A and beyond, the products we back typically have a defined pathway and are moving toward, or through, an FDA submission. This is where regulatory risk usually has the largest effect on valuation, and where most generalist investors have the least ability to actually evaluate it.
  • Growth capital for companies with a defined regulatory pathway

    We deploy larger checks into companies that have moved past the earliest pathway ambiguity and are building toward, or actively in, submission preparation.

  • Co-investment alongside institutional investors

    We invest alongside VC and PE funds as a co-investor bringing regulatory-specific evaluation to the table, complementing generalist commercial and financial diligence rather than duplicating it.

  • Follow-on capacity through regulatory and commercial milestones

    Because the same team that invests also evaluates regulatory milestones directly, follow-on decisions are informed by a genuine read of submission progress, not just a founder update deck.

  • Investment decisions informed by reviewer-side judgment

    Every early-stage investment decision draws on the same reviewer-side FDA experience that anchors Tacticity Advisors' consulting practice, not a generalist read of regulatory risk from the outside.

03 · Diligence Services
Regulatory diligence for funds and family offices, not just for ourselves.
VC, PE, and family office investors evaluating a healthcare or medtech target rarely have in-house reviewer-side expertise. We provide that assessment directly, either for a single deal or as an ongoing empanelled resource across a fund's pipeline.
  • Regulatory and technical diligence for a specific transaction

    A focused assessment of a target's regulatory pathway, classification, predicate strength, and submission readiness, delivered on the timeline a live deal actually requires.

  • FDA compliance history review

    Review of a target's FDA interaction history, including Form 483 observations, Warning Letters, and deficiency responses, to surface compliance risk that standard commercial diligence typically misses.

  • Predicate and pathway risk assessment

    An honest read on whether the target's stated regulatory pathway and predicate strategy will actually hold up under FDA review, before that assumption is priced into the deal.

  • Empanelment for ongoing portfolio-wide diligence

    For funds with recurring healthcare deal flow, we are available as an empanelled diligence resource across the portfolio rather than a one-off engagement rebuilt from scratch each time.

04 · Regulatory Consulting for Portfolio Companies
A regulatory team, not just a check, for the companies we back.
Portfolio companies of Tacticity Capital, and portfolio companies of funds we have diligenced for, get direct access to Tacticity Advisors' full regulatory practice. This is the part most capital cannot offer, and it is available for as long as the company needs it, not just through the round that brought us in.
  • Direct access to Tacticity Advisors' full regulatory practice

    Portfolio companies work directly with the same team behind Tacticity Advisors' consulting practice, from pathway strategy through premarket submission and post-market compliance.

  • Ongoing guidance through FDA milestones

    Regulatory support continues through each FDA milestone, submission, review cycle, deficiency response, rather than ending after an initial diligence exercise.

  • Coordinated support across a fund's portfolio

    For empanelled funds, portfolio companies can be brought into the same regulatory support structure, giving the fund a consistent point of regulatory oversight across multiple holdings.

  • A genuine advantage embedded in the capital itself

    Founders navigating FDA for the first time get investor backing that already understands what that process actually requires, rather than backing that treats regulatory strategy as the founder's problem alone.
