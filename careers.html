Careers

Careful work on technology that reaches patients.

Tacticity works where science, regulation and capital meet. Tacticity Advisors helps medical device, IVD and SaMD companies through U.S. FDA review, and Tacticity Capital invests in early-stage healthcare and medtech companies. Our roles span regulatory science, investments, business and operations, and in every one we look for the same things: rigor, clear thinking and the habit of asking why.

    Why Tacticity

    What you will get from working here.

    01

    Learn from people who have done it

    Work alongside a former FDA CDRH lead reviewer, a CFO with nearly two decades in Indian capital markets, and a scientific advisory board with decades of experience at FDA and in biomaterials research.

    02

    Work that matters

    Whatever your role, your work helps bring medical devices and diagnostics to patients, whether it is a submission going to FDA, an investment decision or a new client relationship.

    03

    Reasons, not routines

    We explain the reasoning behind every recommendation, to clients and to each other. You will be expected to ask why, and to put your thinking in writing clearly.

    04

    Small team, real responsibility

    In a small firm your work is visible and it counts. You will work closely with the leadership team and see how your work shapes the business.

    How We Hire

    Our hiring process, step by step.

    1. Step 01

      Application

      Submit your CV for the role best suited to your skills and experience.

    2. Step 02

      Shortlisting & Confirmation

      Shortlisted candidates receive an email with details about the company and how we work. After reviewing it, reply to confirm your interest, along with:

      • Whether you are currently employed
      • Your current location
      • Your earliest possible joining date

    3. Step 03

      Screening Call

      A 5-minute video call covering your background and your understanding of the company.

    4. Step 04

      Application Review

      We review your application and inform you by email about the next round.

    5. Step 05

      Round 1 Interview

      An in-person interview at our office.

    6. Step 06

      Final Decision

      We communicate the outcome once the process is complete.
